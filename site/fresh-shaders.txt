This file exists only for its response header, Clear-Site-Data: "cache" (site/_headers).

The page (freshShaders in src/main.js) fetches it once, on the first visit after its shaders change.
It works around a Chrome bug: Chrome keeps compiled WebGPU shaders in one 6 MiB in-memory cache per
profile, loads it from disk at startup newest first with older entries pushing newer ones out, and
trims the disk copy only after minutes of uptime. After an update the old version's shaders win, and
every visit compiled the new ones again (about 3 s instead of 0.6), indefinitely. Clearing once
brings loads back to 0.6 s from the third visit. Side effects: it also empties other sites' WebGPU
shader caches in that Chrome profile (Chrome ignores the origin there) and this site's HTTP cache;
saves and settings are untouched. docs/desktop.md "Shader cache" has the measurements and sources.

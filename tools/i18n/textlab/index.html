      Writing a message

      Messages are ICU MessageFormat. Write plain text; only these are special:

      {name}
      A value the page puts in (a species, a number, a time). Click a value under the original to insert it. You may move or drop values; you cannot invent new ones.
      {n, plural, one {# cell} other {# cells}}
      Words that change with a count. # is the number. =0 {none} matches exactly zero.
      {kind, select, grazer {…} other {…}}
      Words that change with a kind. Keep every option the original has (rewrite inside the braces).
      <b>…</b>
      Marked text: bold, a link, a glossary term, a key. Keep the tag around the words it should mark.
      '{' '<' ''
      A literal brace, a less-than sign before a letter, an apostrophe before one of these. An ordinary apostrophe (it's) needs nothing.
      Hints
      A hover hint's text after its last · is shown as the key to press; a second line follows a line break.

    Light and tides feed the world. Tides charge silt into glint; photosynthesising cells drink light directly. Others eat glint, husks or each other, and every cell builds its children out of silt. The dead fall into husks that crumble back into silt. Any kind that spreads over too much of the world sickens with blight. Children sometimes mutate, and every so often the climate turns.

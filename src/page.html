    Tidemote

    WebGPU unavailable

    Tidemote simulates its world on your graphics card through WebGPU, with JavaScript turned on. Try a current Chrome, Edge or Safari, or Firefox on Windows.

    Artificial life, evolving on your GPU

    Tidemote is a sealed biosphere of hundreds of thousands to millions of particles, simulated live in the browser with WebGPU. Cells photosynthesize in the tide's moving light, graze, hunt and scavenge, and build their children out of silt. Children mutate into new species, bonded species grow into multicellular bodies, and the climate turns every 10–18 minutes. Leave it running and it keeps changing.

    Every rule is local: each particle reacts only to what is near it, and the ecology that appears is never steered from above.

    • Food webs of producers, grazers, hunters and scavengers, evolving as you watch
    • A Field Lab of census charts, lineages and a specimen view of any cell
    • Climate eras of light, heat waves, cold snaps and tides
    • An auto camera that films the world, and a soundtrack played by the species themselves
